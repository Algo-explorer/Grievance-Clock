# Four-minute SANGYAN walkthrough

Use only fictional data. Leave AI sharing off for a deterministic offline rehearsal. Enable it only if the API smoke test succeeds and test content is approved for the provider.

1. **0:00–0:30 — Problem.** A worried investor must find the right forum, assemble proof and remember what comes next. Grievance Clock keeps one case from narration to resolution.
2. **0:30–1:30 — Transferred-money scam.** Open the Telegram demo. Point out the immediate 1930/bank prompt. Upload `demo/receipt.txt`. Review facts; explain that extraction is unverified until the user confirms it. Download the dossier.
3. **1:30–2:30 — Act.** Open the demo form portal from the approval dialog. Show the prepared complaint and evidence index. Approval is required. Submit the simulation and show the `DEMO-` reference. Clearly say nothing was sent to a regulator.
4. **2:30–3:30 — Clock.** Open the broker demo, confirm facts and simulate filing. Record the sample entity response with a time after the simulation. Mark it unresolved and prepare SCORES escalation. Simulate that step, then record an entity ATR received via SCORES. The 15-day review clock comes from that response event, not from the chatbot.
5. **3:30–4:00 — Bharat / offline.** Open the transmission demo. Download the physical pack, show the real directory link and explain that the institution supplies the correct official forms. Show language selection, read-aloud and privacy preferences.

Sample response: “We have checked the withdrawal request. It remains pending because our internal reconciliation is incomplete. We cannot provide a completion date yet.”

## Jury talking points

- The ML service is optional. Grievance support works without it.
- The LLM extracts and explains; code controls clocks, routes, approvals and filing status.
- Evidence originals are unchanged, hashed and encrypted. A hash verifies integrity, not authenticity or truth.
- No OTP storage, investment recommendations, speculative prompts or monetisation through broker referrals.
- A filing simulation is labeled at every step. Production portal connectors, messaging delivery, verified identity and branch data are separate integration milestones.

## Submission material

**Problem:** Retail investors often struggle with grievance routes, documents and follow-up dates, especially when interfaces are unfamiliar or language-heavy.

**Solution:** A conversational case workspace with reviewable evidence, prepared documents and source-linked event clocks.

**Impact hypothesis:** Reduce time to a complete complaint and missed follow-ups. Validate using task completion time, routing accuracy on reviewed examples, correction rate after extraction, accessibility feedback and deadline completion rate. No measured impact or recovery rate is claimed yet.

**Scalability:** Replace local ownership with verified identity, SQLite with PostgreSQL, encrypted local files with private object storage, and the in-process checker with a durable worker. Keep case APIs and deterministic rules stable.
